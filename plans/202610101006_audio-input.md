The design was measured before writing this plan (Oct 10, 2026, `spike/RESULTS.md`, "Audio input: host callbacks, binary buffers, the native relay"; scripts in `spike/relay/`). Python 2.7 cannot send a binary, a C++ relay can, and both relays were built, loaded and delivered correct `bytearray`s to a host `processRemote` on both versions.

## Steps

1. **Build files.**
   - `suite.Version` gains `build_files: tuple[PinnedFile, ...]`:
     - 2.1: `naoqi-sdk-2.1.4.13-linux64.tar.gz`.
     - 2.8: `naoqi-sdk-2.8.5.10-linux64.tar.gz`, `libqi-7f3e1394cb26126b26fa7ff54d2de1371a1c9f96.tar.gz` (saved under that name from the fork's `archive/<commit>.tar.gz`), `boost_1_64_0.tar.bz2`.
     - URLs and SHA-256 as in container.md, "Build files".
   - `PinnedFile` takes an optional `filename` for the libqi archive, whose URL ends in `<commit>.tar.gz`.
   - `suite.fetch` downloads them after the suite, with the same keep, `.part`, hash and `hashes.json` rules.
   - Fast tier (`tests/test_suite.py`, fake downloader as today):
     - each version fetches its build files and keeps them on a re-run;
     - a wrong hash fails with `FetchError` naming the file;
     - the libqi archive lands under its pinned name.
2. **The relay source.** `docker/relay/naosim_audiorelay.cpp` from the spike, renamed:
   - module name `_NaoSimAudioRelay`;
   - `deliver(subscriber, nbOfChannels, nbOfSamplesByChannel, timeStamp, buffer)` and `forget(subscriber)`;
   - proxy cache under a mutex;
   - `ALCALL` defined when absent;
   - a header comment pointing to service-replacement.md.
3. **Dockerfiles.** Each becomes three stages (container.md, "The relay's build"):
   - **`naoqi`**: the current content.
   - **`relay`**:
     - `apt-get install g++`;
     - `COPY --from=image-data` the build files;
     - extract only the headers needed: the SDK's `include/` (2.8: plus libqi's `qi/` and `ka/` and boost's `boost/`);
     - compile with the table's flags and include order;
     - link `-L/opt/naoqi/lib -lalcommon -lalvalue -lalerror -lqi -lboost_thread -lboost_system` (2.1: also `-lqitype -lqimessaging`);
     - `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=/opt/naoqi/lib ldd -r` fails the build on any undefined symbol.
   - **Final**: `FROM naoqi`, `COPY --from=relay` the `.so` to `/opt/naoqi/lib/nao-sim/libnaosim_audiorelay.so`.
   - The ignore files let `relay/` in.
   - Check: the recipes digest changes, so `check_images` reports the images outdated.
   - Check: `uv run nao-sim fetch-and-build-images 2.1 2.8` builds both, and a verified boot passes once step 4 lands.
4. **Entrypoints.**
   - `entrypoint-lib.sh` gains:
     - `load_relay <path>`: `launchLocal`, exit 1 when it registers nothing, as `launch_local` does;
     - a `check_answer` call over `ADDED`.
   - Each version's script:
     - sets `RELAY` and `ADDED="ALAudioDevice _NaoSimAudioRelay"`;
     - adds its audio device module to `MODULES`;
     - calls `load_relay` before `load_modules` and checks `ADDED` after `REPLACED`.
   - Compose: `NAO_SIM_HOST_LINK: host.docker.internal:9563` on `naoqi21` and `naoqi28`.
   - Fast tier (`tests/test_entrypoint.py`, fake `qicli`):
     - the sequence on both versions includes the relay's `launchLocal` before the modules and the added-name checks;
     - a relay that registers nothing exits 1;
     - an `ALAudioDevice` that never answers exits 1;
     - neither case reaches `setReady`.
5. **Status key.**
   - `nao_sim_status_core.Status` writes `NaoSim/Audio/Channels` = `none` at boot.
   - `tests/test_status_core.py` checks the keys written at boot.
6. **Host link, `host_link.py`.**
   - `encode(header, payload=b"") -> bytes` and `read_message(sock) -> (header, payload)`, framed `u32 | u32 | JSON | payload`, big-endian.
   - `HostLink(port)`: a `ThreadingTCPServer` on `0.0.0.0:9563`.
     - Each connection's first message must be `hello`; anything else closes it.
     - `register(service, handler)` attaches a handler with `connected(conn)`, `message(header, payload)`, `disconnected()`.
     - A second `hello` for a service replaces (closes) the older connection.
     - `conn.send(header, payload)` is thread-safe.
     - `close()` shuts the server and every connection.
   - Fast tier (`tests/test_host_link.py`):
     - round trip of headers and payloads;
     - a non-`hello` first message is dropped;
     - a second `hello` replaces the first and the handler sees the old connection go;
     - `close()` ends every connection.
7. **The audio device core, `nao_sim_audiodevice_core.py`** (Python 2.7, importable under Python 3: no f-strings or annotations, `socket`/`struct`/`json`/`threading` only).
   - **Preferences.**
     - `parse_preferences(rate, channels, deinterleaved) -> Format`, or `ValueError` for anything audio-device.md refuses.
     - `Format` is a `(rate, channel, deinterleaved)` tuple, with `deinterleaved` false unless all channels.
   - **`AudioDevice(deliver, exists, link_address)`.**
     - `deliver(name, channels, samples, timestamp, data)` is the relay call; `exists(name)` checks a service exists.
     - The methods' logic: `set_preferences`, `subscribe`, `unsubscribe`, energy enable/disable/get.
     - `need()` returns the formats and the energy flag.
   - **`Link`**, the client thread:
     - connects to `NAO_SIM_HOST_LINK`, retrying every second and logging the first failure;
     - on connect, sends `hello` and the current `need`; sends `need` again on every change;
     - routes each `pcm` to the subscribers of its format, stamped `[sec, usec]` on arrival;
     - stores `energy`.
   - **Delivery.**
     - One delivery thread per subscriber, with a queue of 4 (oldest dropped, one log per run).
     - Three consecutive delivery failures unsubscribe the name, log a warning and call `forget`.
   - Fast tier (`tests/test_audiodevice_core.py`):
     - **Against a real `HostLink` (step 6) on a free port**, with a fake `deliver` recording calls:
       - the valid and refused preference combinations (2.1 docs);
       - preferences apply at the next `subscribe` only;
       - the need is the set of distinct formats (two subscribers of one format, one need entry) and goes empty on the last unsubscribe;
       - `need` is re-sent after the host restarts;
       - each `pcm` reaches exactly its format's subscribers with the payload unchanged and an increasing timestamp;
       - energy is `0.0` while disabled and the host's value while enabled.
     - **Robustness:**
       - a subscriber whose `deliver` blocks does not delay another subscriber;
       - three failures unsubscribe it;
       - `subscribe` of a missing name raises;
       - with no host listening, subscribe works and nothing is delivered.
8. **The shells.**
   - **`nao_sim_audiodevice_almodule.py`** (2.1): `ALModule("ALAudioDevice")` with docstring-bound methods (audio-device.md's table, NAO's names and arities).
     - `deliver` through `ALProxy("_NaoSimAudioRelay").deliver`.
     - `exists` through the broker (`ALProxy(name)` construction).
   - **`nao_sim_audiodevice_qiservice.py`** (2.8): a `@qi.multiThreaded()` object registered as `ALAudioDevice` on the module's own session (`NAO_SIM_INTERNAL_PORT`).
     - `deliver` through `session.service("_NaoSimAudioRelay")`.
     - `exists` through `session.services()`.
   - Live checks come in step 13.
9. **The audio input, `audio_input.py`.**
   - **Config first** (`config.py`): `AudioInputSource = Literal["none", "mic", "fake"]`, the `wav` field and its cross-field check removed. `tests/test_config.py`: `fake` loads; `"wav"` as a source and a `wav` key are refused with their key paths.
   - **`FakeAudioSource`** (audio-input.md, "The fake source"):
     - `play(sound, rate=None) -> Take`: a WAV path (via `wave`) or an int16 `numpy` array, mono or 4 channels, 16 or 48 kHz, else `ValueError`; resampled to 48 kHz once, at the call;
     - each `Take` holds its samples and `started_at`; `ended_at` is when its last sample is due; `done` and `wait(timeout)` on an event;
     - `read(t0, n) -> 48 kHz four-channel block` mixes every take overlapping that span (sum, clip to int16), the mono policy applied to mono takes; finished takes are dropped and their events set;
     - `stop()` ends every take now; thread-safe under one lock.
   - **`MicSource`**: a `sounddevice.RawInputStream` (48 kHz, mono, int16, 10 ms blocks), opened by `open()` and closed by `close()`, logging "microphone live"/"off" at info. `check_mic()`: import plus `sd.query_devices(kind="input")`, raising `DeviceUnavailableError`.
   - **`Producer`**:
     - pulls the source on the wall clock into the 48 kHz four-channel base signal (the mic through the mono policy);
     - keeps one resampler state per rate (factor 3, windowed-sinc FIR in `numpy`, continuous across chunks);
     - cuts each needed format's stream at 4096 (48 kHz) or 1365 (16 kHz) samples per channel, picks or deinterleaves channels, and paces each stream on its own clock;
     - computes the energy RMS over each 4096-sample block while needed.
   - **Gate** (both sources): a 10 ms block whose time falls while `time.monotonic() < audio_output.playing_until + gate_tail_s` becomes zeros.
   - **`AudioInput(source, link, audio_output, gate_tail_s, connect)`**, the `HostLink` handler for `ALAudioDevice`:
     - `need` starts, retargets or stops the producer; a fresh need opens a `MicSource` and no need closes it;
     - a `need` before `start()` is kept;
     - `start()` writes `NaoSim/Audio/Source` and `NaoSim/Audio/Channels` over qi (`stack.connect`, with its retry);
     - `stop()` stops the producer, closes the mic, stops the fake source's takes and writes `none` to both keys (best effort).
   - Fast tier (`tests/test_audio_input.py`):
     - **The fake source:**
       - with nothing played, every chunk is zeros at the normal cadence;
       - a 16 kHz mono tone played and served as 16 kHz front comes back sample-exact in 1365-sample chunks;
       - served as 48 kHz all channels it is duplicated with `duplicate` and on front only with `silence`;
       - a 4-channel array keeps each channel in NAO order;
       - two overlapping takes are mixed (and clipped);
       - a take played before any need is partly or wholly unheard, by its wall-clock time;
       - `ended_at` matches its duration and `wait` returns then;
       - `stop()` cuts every take;
       - wrong formats (8-bit WAV, stereo, 44.1 kHz, float array, missing file) raise `ValueError` naming the problem.
     - **Formats:**
       - a 48 kHz tone served at 16 kHz keeps its frequency (FFT peak) with no click at chunk boundaries;
       - deinterleaved layout;
       - two formats needed at once each get their own stream;
       - energy is near the tone's RMS during it and near 0 after.
     - **Timing:**
       - pacing gives 10 to 12 chunks of 85 ms over 1 s and never bursts after a slow consumer;
       - a need received before `start()` is served after it;
       - the keys are written at start and reset at stop.
     - **Gate:**
       - while a fake audio output's `playing_until` is in the future (plus the tail), a playing take reaches the link as zeros at the same cadence, and as itself after.
     - **Mic** (`fake_sounddevice` grows a `RawInputStream`):
       - the mic opens on the first need and closes on the last, and its blocks are gated the same way;
       - `check_mic` with no input device raises `DeviceUnavailableError`.

13. **Live tests, `test_audio_input_live.py`** (both versions, default live config):
    - `ALAudioDevice` and `_NaoSimAudioRelay` are registered;
    - `NaoSim/Audio/Source` = `fake`, `NaoSim/Audio/Channels` = `duplicate`.
    - **The shape of what arrives:**
      - a subscriber at 16 kHz front receives `bytearray` buffers of 1365 samples, one channel, zeros while nothing plays;
      - about 11.7 chunks per second (± 2 over 2 s), timestamps increasing by about 85 ms;
      - a 440 Hz tone played for 1 s, then a 1 kHz tone 0.5 s after it ends (split takes), arrive in that order with those pitches (FFT peak per window), each within 150 ms of its `started_at`;
      - the default preferences give 48 kHz four channels, interleaved, 4096 samples, all channels equal;
      - deinterleaved gives four equal blocks;
      - a 16 kHz and a 48 kHz subscriber at once both get their streams.
    - **The gate on every run:** a tone played across a robot `say` arrives as zeros from the speech's start (within 100 ms) until its end plus `gate_tail_s` (within 150 ms), and as the tone before and after (the speech's timing read from the `MemorySink`).
    - **Methods:**
      - energy enabled reads high during a tone and low without one;
      - `unsubscribe` stops delivery within 200 ms;
      - a refused combination raises;
      - a subscriber whose session closes without unsubscribing is dropped and another keeps receiving.
    - `test_status_live.py`: `NaoSim/Audio/Channels` reads as the audio input set it.
